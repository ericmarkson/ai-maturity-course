# Topic Seed List — Levels 1-10

Source: generated in chat on 2026-07-13, approved by user as the basis for the syllabus. This supersedes the original narrower "evals/tracing/autonomous systems only" scope from PLAN.md v1 — those topics are folded in naturally at levels 6-9 below, but the site now covers all of AI, beginner to master.

## Level 1 — Beginner
What AI/ML/deep learning actually are and how they differ; narrow vs. general AI; core vocabulary (model, training, inference, dataset, parameters); everyday AI applications (recommendations, voice assistants, chatbots); what a neural network is, conceptually; basic AI ethics (bias, privacy).

## Level 2 — Novice
Supervised vs. unsupervised vs. reinforcement learning; basic probability/statistics for ML; linear and logistic regression; classification vs. regression; train/validation/test splits; overfitting vs. underfitting; using AI tools/APIs (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.); basic prompt engineering.

## Level 3 — Advanced Beginner
Python for ML (numpy, pandas); decision trees, random forests; k-NN, k-means clustering; feature engineering and data cleaning; perceptrons and basic network architecture; gradient descent; loss functions.

## Level 4 — Intermediate
Backpropagation and activation functions; CNNs for images; RNNs/LSTMs for sequences; intro NLP (tokenization, embeddings, word2vec); evaluation metrics (precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC); regularization (dropout, L1/L2); PyTorch/TensorFlow basics; transfer learning.

## Level 5 — Intermediate+
Transformer architecture and attention; pretrained language models (BERT/GPT-style); fine-tuning vs. prompting; embeddings and vector search; intro reinforcement learning (Q-learning, policy gradients); hyperparameter tuning; basic model deployment/serving; MLOps fundamentals.

## Level 6 — Advanced
LLM architecture in depth (self-attention, positional encoding, tokenizers); advanced prompting (few-shot, chain-of-thought); retrieval-augmented generation (RAG); fine-tuning methods (LoRA, QLoRA, full fine-tune); intro to evals for ML/LLM systems; interpretability tools (SHAP, LIME); distributed training basics.

## Level 7 — Advanced+
Agentic systems (tool use, function calling, ReAct); multi-agent orchestration; agent memory (short/long-term); advanced RAG (hybrid search, reranking, chunking); tracing/observability for AI systems; RLHF/RLAIF and alignment techniques; quantization and distillation; scaling laws.

## Level 8 — Expert
Autonomous agent architecture (planning, self-correction, guardrails); production ML systems at scale; model safety and red-teaming; constitutional AI and alignment methods; mixture-of-experts architectures; multi-modal models (vision-language, audio); pretraining considerations; rigorous eval design (LLM-as-judge, statistical significance).

## Level 9 — Expert+ / Research-Adjacent
Novel architectures (state-space models, Mamba, etc.); mechanistic interpretability research; AI safety/alignment theory and scalable oversight; distributed systems for frontier-scale training; synthetic data generation and curation; reward model design; long-context architectures; cross-disciplinary AI (biology, robotics, science).

## Level 10 — Master
Frontier model pretraining and research; original contributions to alignment/safety paradigms; novel theoretical contributions (papers, architectures); designing eval and safety benchmarks for the field; AGI-adjacent research directions; advising policy/governance on frontier AI; leading research organizations or labs; defining state of the art in a subfield.
