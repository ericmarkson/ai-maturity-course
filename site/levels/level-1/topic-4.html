Level 1 — Beginner

Everyday AI Applications

Learning objective: Learner can identify at least 5 AI-powered features they personally use in a typical day and name the category each falls into.

Definition

AI already runs quietly inside many of the tools people use every day, often without any "AI" label in sight. It shows up as recommendation feeds deciding what you see next, voice assistants turning speech into action, conversational chatbots you type or talk to directly, and a whole category of "behind-the-scenes" AI — fraud detection, spam filtering, autocomplete, navigation ETAs — working invisibly in the background of ordinary apps.

Subtopics

  • Recommendation systems (streaming, shopping, social feeds)
  • Voice assistants and speech recognition
  • Conversational chatbots / assistants
  • Behind-the-scenes AI (fraud detection, spam filtering, autocomplete, navigation ETAs)

The categories matter because they describe different relationships with the user. Recommendation systems and behind-the-scenes AI act on your behalf without being asked in the moment. Voice assistants and chatbots, by contrast, respond to an explicit request — you talk or type, and the system reacts directly. Recognizing which bucket a given feature falls into is a fast way to spot AI you might otherwise never notice.
