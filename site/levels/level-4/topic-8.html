Level 4 — Intermediate

Transfer Learning

Learning objective: Explain why a model pretrained on general images can be adapted to a narrow task (e.g. classifying a specific product type) with much less data than training from scratch, and describe the tradeoff between freezing vs. fine-tuning layers.

Definition

Transfer learning means reusing a model that's already been trained on a large, general dataset as the starting point for a new, related task — instead of training a new model from scratch. The early layers of a pretrained network tend to have learned general-purpose features (edges, textures, common word patterns); the later layers tend to be more specific to the original task. Transfer learning reuses the general part and adapts the specific part.

Subtopics

  • Why training large models from scratch is expensive and often unnecessary
  • Freezing early layers (keep their general features fixed) vs. fine-tuning later layers (adapt task-specific features)
  • Feature extraction vs. full fine-tuning as two ends of a spectrum
  • When transfer learning works well vs. when the source and target domains are too different for it to help
