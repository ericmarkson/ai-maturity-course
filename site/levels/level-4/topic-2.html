CNNs for Images

Learning objective: Manually apply a small (3x3) filter to a toy pixel grid and compute the resulting feature-map value at one position, and explain why weight-sharing makes CNNs far more efficient than a fully-connected network for images.

Definition

A Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) is a network architecture specialized for images. It uses small sliding filters ("kernels") that detect local patterns like edges and textures, then stacks those detected patterns into increasingly complex shapes across layers.

Subtopics

  • Convolution as a sliding filter/kernel over pixels
  • Feature maps and stacking layers to go from edges to shapes to objects
  • Pooling (downsampling) to reduce size while keeping important information
  • Why CNNs share weights across an image instead of having one weight per pixel
