Level 4 — Intermediate

Regularization: Dropout, L1/L2

Learning objective: Look at a plot of training vs. validation loss and identify signs of overfitting, then explain how adding dropout or an L2 penalty would be expected to change that plot.

Definition

Regularization is a set of techniques that deliberately handicap a model during training so it learns general patterns instead of memorizing the training data. A model with plenty of capacity can always drive training error toward zero by memorizing noise — regularization trades away a little training performance for much better performance on data it hasn't seen.

Subtopics

  • Overfitting recap and regularization as the fix
  • L1/L2 penalties: adding a cost for large weights to the loss function so the model prefers smaller, simpler weights
  • Dropout: randomly disabling neurons during training so the network can't over-rely on any single one of them
  • The regularization-strength tradeoff: too little and the model overfits; too much and it underfits
