Level 4 — Intermediate

RNNs and LSTMs for Sequences

Learning objective: Trace, step by step, how a hidden state updates as a short toy sequence is fed through an RNN one item at a time, and explain in plain language what an LSTM's "forget gate" is protecting against.

Definition

Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) process sequences — like text or time series — one step at a time while carrying a "memory" (hidden state) forward. LSTMs (Long Short-Term Memory networks) are an improved RNN design with gates that decide what to keep vs. discard, making them much better at remembering things over long sequences.

Subtopics

  • The hidden state as memory passed from one time step to the next
  • Why plain RNNs struggle with long sequences (vanishing gradients over time)
  • LSTM gates (forget/input/output) at a conceptual "what to keep vs. discard" level
  • Sequence-to-sequence intuition (e.g., predicting the next word)
