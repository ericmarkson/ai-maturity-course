Original Contributions to Alignment/Safety Paradigms

Learning objective: Draft an original (even if modest) alignment technique or failure-mode hypothesis, and pressure-test it by articulating the strongest counterargument against your own idea.

Definition

Original contributions to alignment/safety paradigms means proposing new frameworks or techniques for keeping advanced AI systems safe and aligned — not just applying existing ones like RLHF or constitutional AI, but identifying a genuinely new failure mode, mitigation, or theoretical framing and then stress-testing it under real scrutiny.

Subtopics

  • The landscape of existing paradigms — RLHF/RLAIF, constitutional AI, debate, and weak-to-strong generalization as a baseline to build on or critique.
  • What counts as a genuine contribution — a new failure mode identified, a new mitigation technique, or a new theoretical framing (e.g., reward hacking as objective compression plus optimization amplification).
  • Publishing and stress-testing an idea — red-teaming your own proposal, adversarial review, and reproducibility.
  • Model-welfare and superintelligence-governance framing entering mainstream alignment discourse in 2026.
