Fine-Tuning vs. Prompting

Learning objective: Given a scenario (budget, data volume, task specificity), recommend prompting, few-shot prompting, or fine-tuning and justify the trade-off.

Definition

Fine-tuning changes a model's internal weights to specialize it for a task, while prompting just changes the instructions/examples fed to a frozen model at inference time. Fine-tuning can produce a more reliable specialist but costs data, compute, and ongoing maintenance; prompting is cheap and instant to change but leans on the model's existing general knowledge.

Subtopics

  • When prompting is enough vs. when fine-tuning is worth the cost
  • Few-shot prompting as a lightweight alternative to fine-tuning
  • Cost, latency, and maintenance trade-offs (data needs, retraining, versioning)
  • Risk of overfitting or catastrophic forgetting when fine-tuning
