Embeddings and Vector Search

Learning objective: Explain why two differently-worded sentences with the same meaning end up near each other in embedding space, and manually compute/compare similarity between simple toy vectors.

Definition

Embeddings turn words, sentences, or documents into lists of numbers (vectors) positioned so that similar meanings end up close together in that number-space, enabling "search by meaning" instead of exact keyword match. Two phrases can share zero words in common and still land near each other in embedding space if they mean similar things.

Subtopics

  • What a vector embedding represents geometrically (distance = similarity)
  • Cosine similarity / dot product as a similarity measure
  • Vector databases/indexes at a conceptual level (why brute-force search doesn't scale)
  • Semantic search vs. keyword search
