Basic Probability and Statistics for ML

Learning objective: Read a model's output like "87% confidence this is spam" and correctly explain what that percentage represents and why it isn't a guarantee.

Definition

Machine learning leans on a small toolbox of statistics ideas to describe data and quantify uncertainty: probability (how likely something is, from 0 to 1), the mean and variance of a dataset (its center and spread), and correlation (whether two variables tend to move together — which is not the same thing as one causing the other).

Subtopics

  • Probability basics: the likelihood of an event, expressed as 0–1 or a percentage
  • Mean, median, and variance/spread of a dataset
  • Correlation (two variables moving together) vs. causation
  • Why ML predictions are often probabilities/confidence scores, not certainties
