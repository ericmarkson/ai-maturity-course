Overfitting vs. Underfitting

Learning objective: Look at a plotted curve fit to sample data points and correctly diagnose it as overfit, underfit, or well-fit.

Definition

Overfitting happens when a model memorizes the training data too closely, including its noise, and then fails on new data. Underfitting happens when a model is too simple to capture the real pattern, so it performs poorly even on the data it was trained on. The goal is a model complex enough to capture the true pattern but not so complex it starts fitting noise.

Subtopics

  • The "just right" fit vs. an overly wiggly curve (overfitting) vs. an overly flat curve (underfitting)
  • Symptoms: overfitting shows great training accuracy but poor test accuracy; underfitting shows poor accuracy everywhere
  • Common causes: too little data, too complex a model, or training too long (overfitting); too simple a model (underfitting)
  • Basic intuition for the fix direction: simplify vs. add capacity/data
