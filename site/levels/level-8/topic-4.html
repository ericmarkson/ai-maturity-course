Constitutional AI and Alignment Methods

Learning objective: Given a sample constitution (a short list of principles) and a draft model response, identify which principle the response violates and describe what a revised response should look like.

Definition

Constitutional AI is an alignment approach where a model is trained to critique and revise its own outputs against a written set of principles (a "constitution"), reducing reliance on large-scale human-labeled preferences. Instead of a human rating every response, the model learns to check its own draft against explicit rules and produce a better version.

Subtopics

  • The constitution — explicit written principles guiding model behavior
  • Self-critique and revision loops — the model checks its own draft against the principles
  • Reduced (not eliminated) reliance on human labels — the constitution stands in for some preference data
  • Complementary to RLHF/RLAIF — constitutional AI works alongside, not instead of, human/AI feedback training
