Pretraining Considerations

Learning objective: List at least three data-quality decisions that must be made before pretraining begins, and explain why the data mixture (e.g. percentage of code vs. web text vs. books) affects downstream model behavior.

Definition

Pretraining considerations are the major design decisions made before training a foundation model from scratch, covering what data to use, how to prepare it, and what objective to train against. These choices are made once, at huge cost, so getting them wrong is expensive to correct later.

Subtopics

  • Data curation and mixture — sourcing, deduplication, filtering, and balancing domains/languages
  • Data contamination and quality control — avoiding test-set leakage, toxic or low-quality content
  • Pretraining objectives — e.g. next-token prediction, and tokenizer design choices
  • Compute/infrastructure planning — checkpointing, data pipeline throughput tied to pretraining decisions
