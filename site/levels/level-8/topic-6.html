Multi-Modal Models: Vision-Language and Audio

Learning objective: Describe, at a conceptual level, how an image and a text prompt both end up as inputs a transformer can process, and give one example use case each for vision-language and audio-language models.

Definition

Multi-modal models are AI systems that can process and/or generate more than one type of data — such as text, images, and audio — within a single model. Each modality is encoded into vectors that live in a shared representation (embedding) space, so a transformer can reason over text, image, and audio content side by side.

Subtopics

  • Shared representation space — how different modalities get encoded into vectors the model can compare
  • Vision-language models — image understanding, captioning, visual question answering
  • Audio modalities — speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and native audio understanding
  • Fusion strategies — early vs. late fusion of modalities, and cross-modal attention
